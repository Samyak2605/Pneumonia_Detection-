### Card 1: Title & Executive Overview
**Pediatric Pneumonia Detection: From Dense Overfitting to Parameter-Efficient Global Average Pooling**
*Milestone 2 Replication & Milestone 3 Controlled Architectural Innovation*

- **The Clinical Challenge**: Pediatric pneumonia remains the #1 infectious killer of children under 5 worldwide. Rapid, automated chest X-ray screening can save lives in underserved clinics.
- **The Research Question**: Does a 4-layer CNN need 9.68 Million parameters, or is 97.5% of its capacity wasted on spatial overfitting?
- **Our Contribution**:
  - Replicated base paper **Stephen et al. (2019)** on 5,856 Kermany radiographs.
  - Formulated a controlled hypothesis replacing `Flatten + Dense(512)` with `GlobalAveragePooling2D`.
  - Achieved **+1.78% higher validation accuracy** while eliminating **97.51% of model parameters** (down to 240k) and shrinking disk size from 37 MB to 0.92 MB.
- **Key Takeaway**: Bigger is not better in medical imaging—structural inductive bias improves both accuracy and clinical interpretability.

### Card 2: Problem Statement — The Dual Challenge (Clinical & Architectural)
**Why Existing Scratch-Trained CNNs Fail: The Overfitting Bottleneck**

- **Clinical Problem (Pediatric Radiography)**:
  - Pediatric chest X-rays suffer from motion blur, small lung volumes, and prominent thymus silhouettes.
  - Radiologist shortages in rural clinics cause fatal diagnostic delays or severe antibiotic overtreatment.
- **The Architectural Bottleneck in Stephen et al. (2019)**:
  - Base CNN extracts features via 4 Conv layers, producing a 12x12x128 feature map (18,432 activations).
  - The `Flatten()` head directly feeds into a `Dense(512)` layer: $(18,432 \times 512) + 512 = \mathbf{9,437,696\text{ parameters}}$.
  - **Over 97.5% of total network weights reside in a single dense layer!**
- **The Failure Mode**:
  - The model memorizes exact pixel coordinates of rib edges, diaphragms, and patient positioning markers instead of genuine pulmonary pathology.
  - Generalization gap: Training loss drops to zero while validation loss diverges after epoch 12.
*Visual suggestion: Split comparison layout showing clinical X-ray challenge on the left and a stacked bar showing 97.5% parameter bloat in Dense(512) on the right.*

### Card 3: Proposed Solution — Controlled Architectural Reform (GAP-CNN)
**Replacing Dense Memorization with Global Average Pooling (GAP)**

- **The Controlled Hypothesis**:
  *"Replacing the large Flatten + Dense(512) classification head with GlobalAveragePooling2D will reduce overfitting by decreasing trainable parameters (>97%), leading to improved validation F1-score and AUC while maintaining accuracy and enabling native Grad-CAM interpretability."*
- **The Controlled Architectural Transformation**:
  - **Identical Backbone**: Same 4 Conv2D blocks (32, 64, 128, 128 filters) held strictly constant (240,832 params).
  - **Classification Head Reform**:
    - **Baseline**: `Flatten(18,432) -> Dropout(0.5) -> Dense(512) -> Dense(1)` [9,438,209 params]
    - **Proposed**: `GlobalAveragePooling2D(128) -> Dropout(0.3) -> Dense(1, sigmoid)` [**129 params!**]
- **Mathematical Inductive Bias**:
  - GAP calculates the spatial mean $\bar{f}_k = \frac{1}{H \times W} \sum_{i,j} f_k(i, j)$ for each feature map.
  - Eliminates position-dependent dense weights and enforces spatial translation invariance across lung fields.
- **Classification Head Reduction**: **99.998% reduction** in head parameters (9.44M $\to$ 129).
*Visual suggestion: Architectural flow diagram showing the identical 4-stage convolutional backbone branching into the bulky Flatten+Dense head vs the sleek GAP head.*

### Card 6: Deployment Feasibility, Limitations & Milestone 4 Roadmap
**From Academic Benchmark to Point-of-Care Pediatric Diagnostic Tool**

- **Edge Deployment Readiness**:
  - At **0.92 MB**, the model fits entirely into L3 cache of low-cost mobile processors.
  - Capable of running offline on handheld ultrasound devices, Android tablets, and portable digital X-ray vans without internet or cloud APIs.
- **Current Limitations**:
  - Single-center dataset (Guangzhou pediatric cohort).
  - Binary classification: Does not differentiate between viral and bacterial pneumonia.
- **Milestone 4 (M4) Strategic Roadmap**:
  1. **3-Class Etiological Subtyping**: Distinguish Normal vs. Bacterial vs. Viral pneumonia to drive antibiotic stewardship.
  2. **Cross-Center External Validation**: Validate generalization on external clinical cohorts (CheXpert and NIH ChestX-ray14).
  3. **INT8 Quantization**: Compress model via TensorFlow Lite to **~240 KB** with zero loss in sensitivity for embedded microcontrollers.
- **Final Verdict**: Milestone 3 hypothesis is fully validated. Parameter reduction improves generalization, inference speed, and clinical explainability.
